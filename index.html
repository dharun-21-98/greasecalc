Assumptions & engineering limitations
  1. The free-volume equation shown is intended for bearings with a metallic cage.
  2. For non-metallic cages, obtain bearing-specific guidance or internal free-volume data.
  3. Housing grease quantities are estimates — actual housing cavity dimensions are not entered.
  4. SKF plummer-block housings are generally only partly filled; follow the applicable housing instructions and selected filling percentage.
  5. Relubrication quantity and interval depend on speed, temperature, contamination, orientation, sealing, grease type and operating conditions.
  6. All outputs are approximate engineering estimates and should be verified before use.
